Advanced Memory Formula Ingredients

The quality of any brain supplement hinges entirely on its advanced memory formula ingredients list. Here is what the formula contains and what each ingredient does.

Huperzine A
Acetylcholine Protector

Huperzine A inhibits acetylcholinesterase, the enzyme that breaks down acetylcholine in the brain. By slowing this breakdown, it allows higher concentrations of this critical memory neurotransmitter to remain active at synapses. Clinical evidence indicates that Huperzine A can measurably improve memory performance in older adults.

Bacopa Monnieri
Memory Consolidation

Bacopa is an Ayurvedic herb with a strong research profile for memory enhancement. Studies show it improves the rate of learning and helps the brain consolidate new information into long-term memory. It also contains potent antioxidants called bacosides that protect neurons from oxidative damage.

Ginkgo Biloba
Cerebrovascular Flow

Ginkgo Biloba is among the most studied herbs for brain health. Research suggests it improves blood microcirculation to the brain, enhancing oxygen and nutrient delivery to memory centers. It also has antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties that protect against neurodegeneration over time.

Phosphatidylserine
Neuron Membrane Integrity

Phosphatidylserine is a phospholipid that forms a critical component of neuron cell membranes. As we age, membrane integrity declines, impairing signal transmission between cells. Research suggests supplementing with phosphatidylserine can improve cognitive scores, particularly for age-related memory decline and processing speed.

Vinpocetine
Oxygen and Glucose Delivery

Derived from the periwinkle plant, Vinpocetine enhances cerebral blood flow and improves the brain's utilization of oxygen and glucose. Clinical studies indicate it may sharpen concentration and working memory, particularly in individuals with mild cognitive impairment. It also has neuroprotective properties against excitotoxicity.

Alpha GPC
Choline Precursor

Alpha GPC delivers choline directly to the brain, where it is converted into acetylcholine. It is considered one of the most bioavailable choline sources available. Research suggests it supports mental focus, memory encoding, and may have neuroprotective effects that slow age-related cognitive decline when used consistently.

Lion's Mane Mushroom
BDNF and NGF Stimulator

Lion's Mane contains compounds called hericenones and erinacines that stimulate Nerve Growth Factor (NGF) production in the brain. NGF supports the survival, growth, and maintenance of neurons. Studies show Lion's Mane extract can improve mild cognitive impairment and supports long-term brain plasticity.

L-Theanine
Calm Focus Enhancer

L-Theanine is an amino acid found in green tea that promotes relaxed alertness without sedation. It increases alpha brain wave activity, which is associated with a calm and focused mental state. Research suggests it works synergistically with other nootropic compounds to enhance attention and reduce stress-driven cognitive interference.
